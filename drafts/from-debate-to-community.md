## Where we started

new.vote began life as debate.report: a place for structured, evidence-led debate, where every argument sits in a visible map of claims, evidence and rebuttals, and anyone can follow how a conclusion was reached. That engine is still at the heart of what we do.

But the groups that debate also need to decide, organise and keep their conversations their own. Over the last few days we've rebuilt new.vote around them. It is now a home for communities online, which we call **Societies**, each run by its own members and moderators.

## Societies, and a toolkit to run them

Every Society can be **public**, open to **apply** for membership, or **invitation only**. Each one gets a full set of tools, and its moderators can switch off anything it doesn't need, so a Society can be as simple or as full-featured as its members want:

- **Debate**: the structured debates new.vote is known for, with votes on every point and quality checks that flag unsupported claims and possible fallacies.
- **Polls**: secret or named ballots; single and multiple choice, ranking, ranked-choice voting with a true instant runoff, star ratings, scales and word clouds; quizzes; and a live presentation mode for meetings.
- **News**: a regular magazine of stories on the topics your Society cares about, gathered from mainstream and alternative sources and organised under your own headings.
- **Discussions**: members-only threads, with one-click reporting of anything that crosses a line.
- **Meet**: audio and video rooms, with optional end-to-end encryption for sensitive conversations. Calls are never recorded.
- **Documents**: shared files and links in folders.
- **Events**: the Society's calendar, with one-click "add to my calendar".
- **Projects**: tasks, status reports and a log of risks and issues.
- **Principles**: the rules a Society runs by, published in versions that members can question, discuss and debate.

## Ballots you can trust

We've taken voting seriously. In a secret poll, the record of who voted is kept apart from the ballots themselves, so not even a copy of our database can tie a ballot to a voter. Every voter gets a receipt to check their ballot was counted, and moderators can recount every ballot from scratch with one click. People without an account can vote with a one-use access code.

## Private means private

Privacy is built in, not bolted on:

- Everything a private Society creates (its debates, polls, discussions, documents, events, projects and principles) is stored **encrypted with that Society's own key**.
- The master key that unlocks those keys can be **held offline**, never stored on our servers.
- Our administrators have **no routine access** to private Societies. In a genuine emergency they can open time-limited access, but only with a reason, and every such access is logged and shown to the Society.
- No ads, and no tracking.

## AI, on your terms

Some tools, such as debate quality reviews and news summaries, can use AI. Each Society chooses new.vote's AI, its own AI provider, or none at all, and can set limits on what it spends.

## Where we are: Alpha

new.vote is now in **Alpha testing**. Things will change quickly, and some rough edges remain; that's what this stage is for. We expect to move to **Beta in December**, and to be **fully open for business on 1 January 2027**.

## What's next: the most private community app

We're expecting to collaborate with a development team working on a new protocol for maximum security and privacy. Together, we plan to make new.vote the most secure and private community app available, and to bring **self-hosting** and **federation** options, so communities can run new.vote on their own servers and connect with each other. We're aiming for the first quarter of 2027.
